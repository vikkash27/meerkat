# Demo script: "The Pitch"

**Setup:** two laptops on one Google Meet call.
- **Investor (Sam):** runs BS Detector, wears headphones, and shares their screen with the audience so everyone sees the 💩. Fact-checking is one-way: only the founder is checked. Sam's own lines are context and never get flagged.
- **Founder (Alex):** the other laptop. They can't see any of it.

Speak clearly and **pause about 5 seconds after each lie**. A check took 2–4 seconds in testing, then the 💩 burst lands around Alex's face along with the correction.

---

**SAM:** Alex, thanks for jumping on. Tell me about the company.

**ALEX:** Thanks, Sam. We're building AI copilots for logistics teams. We started like Apple, which was founded in a garage in 1976. That's literally how we started.
> ✅ *Checks out.* Shows that true claims pass.

**SAM:** Love a garage story. Who's on the team?

**ALEX:** Our CTO is incredible. She was the first woman to walk on the Moon, so she knows moonshots.
> 💩 *No woman has walked on the Moon* (NASA).

**SAM:** …Okay. How big is the market?

**ALEX:** Huge. The global AI market is already worth fifty trillion dollars.
> 💩 *About $0.5–2.7 trillion, not $50 trillion.*

**SAM:** And traction? Any notable advisors?

**ALEX:** I just had a meeting with Muhammad Ali yesterday, and he loved the product. He's going to be an advisor.
> 💩 *Muhammad Ali died in 2016.*

**SAM:** (to camera) One-way, by the way. If I say the Eiffel Tower was built in 1999, nothing happens. Only the founder gets checked.
> *(no burst)*

**ALEX:** Fun fact, the Empire State Building is still the tallest building in the world.
> 💩 *It hasn't been the tallest since the 1970s.*

**SAM:** Alex, I think we've seen enough. (Points at the 💩 score in the panel.)

## Before you go on stage
- Open the Meet call in Chrome on Sam's Mac. BS Detector starts listening by itself (🔴 💩 in the menu bar). **Swap Me / Them Faces** if the burst lands on the wrong tile.
- Rehearse the visuals with **Test BS Burst**. Rehearse the checks with the 💩 menu → **Also Fact-check Me (rehearsal)** and read Alex's lines yourself.
- Each line above was checked through the live pipeline on 11 Nov 2026 and got the verdict shown. The models aren't deterministic, so do one dry run before the demo.
