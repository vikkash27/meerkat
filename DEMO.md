# Demo script: "The Pitch"

A founder, Alex, is raising a seed round and lies his way through the pitch. The investor, Sam, has BS Detector running, and it exposes him live.

**Setup:** two laptops on one Google Meet call.
- **Sam (Investor):** runs BS Detector, wears headphones, and shares their screen so the audience sees Meet with the BS Detector panel. The panel transcribes **both** people, labelled *Investor* and *Founder*. Only the founder is fact-checked.
- **Alex (Founder):** the other laptop. He can't see any of it.

**Delivery:** say each 🎯 line word for word, then **pause about 5 seconds**. In testing a verdict took 1.5–3 seconds, then the 💩 burst lands around Alex's face with the correction.

---

### 1. Warm-up

**SAM:** Hey Alex, thanks for making the time. I've got twenty minutes. Tell me what you're building.

**ALEX:** Thanks Sam. We're building AI copilots for freight and logistics teams. Honestly, it's a lot like the early days of Apple. 🎯 *"Apple was founded in a garage in 1976, and that's exactly how we started."*
> ✅ **Checks out.** The audience sees that it's fair and true claims pass.

**SAM:** Ha, classic. Why now?

**ALEX:** 🎯 *"OpenAI launched ChatGPT in November 2022, and that's when we knew this was the moment."*
> ✅ **Checks out.**

### 2. The lies start

**SAM:** Makes sense. Tell me about the team.

**ALEX:** Our team is world-class. 🎯 *"Our CTO was the first woman to walk on the Moon, so she knows moonshots."*
> 💩 **BULLSHIT:** no woman has walked on the Moon yet (NASA).

**SAM:** …Right. How big is the opportunity?

**ALEX:** Massive. 🎯 *"The global AI market is already worth fifty trillion dollars."*
> 💩 **BULLSHIT:** it's around $0.5–2.7 trillion.

**SAM:** And who are you up against?

**ALEX:** Amazon, basically. 🎯 *"Amazon only made about a billion dollars in revenue last year."* (pause) We're catching up fast.
> 💩 **BULLSHIT:** Amazon's revenue is around $700 billion.

### 3. The name-drops

**SAM:** Bold. Who else is involved? Investors, advisors?

**ALEX:** We're very selective. 🎯 *"Y Combinator backed us, and they've only ever funded ten companies in their whole history."*
> 💩 **BULLSHIT:** YC has funded more than 5,000 companies. "YC backed us" shows as 🤷 *can't verify*, because it's a private claim.

**SAM:** Any big names on board?

**ALEX:** Oh, for sure. 🎯 *"I just had a meeting with Muhammad Ali yesterday, and he loved the product."*
> 💩 **BULLSHIT:** Muhammad Ali died in 2016.

### 4. The close

**SAM:** (turning to the audience) Alex, I'm going to stop you there. (Points at the panel.) Founder: 💩 5. I don't think we'll be leading this round.

**ALEX:** …Can I at least tell you about our office in the Empire State Building? 🎯 *"Fun fact, the Empire State Building is still the tallest building in the world."*
> 💩 **BULLSHIT:** it hasn't been since the 1970s. The Burj Khalifa is. *(Closing laugh.)*

## Before you go on stage
- On Sam's Mac, open the Meet call in Chrome. Open **Meerkat — Call Controls** and click **Start listening** (🔴 💩 in the menu bar). Use **Swap Investor / Founder Faces** if the burst lands on Sam's tile instead of Alex's.
- Rehearse the visuals with **Test BS Burst**. To rehearse alone, use the 💩 menu → **Also Fact-check Investor (rehearsal)** and read Alex's lines yourself.
- Every 🎯 line was run through the live pipeline on 11 Nov 2026 and got the verdict shown. The models aren't fully deterministic, so do one full dry run before the demo.
- The app is signed with a local identity, so rebuilds keep the Screen Recording permission. Grant it once.
